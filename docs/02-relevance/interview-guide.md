# Interview guide

Duration: 15–25 minutes. Open, non-leading questions. Read the consent text first.

## Respondent profile (anonymised)
- Role, organisation type, number of meetings per week, languages used in meetings

## Questions

Ask open questions first, then use the probes only when relevant.

1. How are decisions and tasks recorded after a meeting today? Who prepares the protocol, and how long does it usually take?
2. What makes a protocol usable or unusable for you? Which details are most often missed?
3. Which languages are used in your meetings? How often do speakers switch between Kazakh and Russian, and how should names or technical terms be represented?
4. What confidentiality rules apply to meeting audio, transcripts and action items? Which data may not leave the organisation, and what approval is needed to process it?
5. How are action items assigned, verified and followed up? What errors or ambiguities occur most often?
6. What would make you trust or distrust an automatically generated transcript, summary or action item?
7. Would audio quotes linked to transcript segments help you verify a decision or assignment? What would make that workflow safe and practical?
8. What would you need to correct before exporting a protocol, and which export format is useful?

## Closing
- Anything we did not ask that matters?
- May we contact you again for a prototype test?
