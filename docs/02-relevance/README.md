# Evidence of relevance

## Current research status

Before pre-defense 1, no in-depth interviews with target users had been conducted. The repository therefore does not present interview results as completed evidence. The current relevance claims are hypotheses to be tested, not findings.

## Formal interview plan

At least three semi-structured interviews will be completed before pre-defense 2, with one respondent from each of these target roles:

1. A secretary of a rectorate or dean's office, who prepares formal meeting records.
2. A CISO or IT officer at a bank, who can describe confidentiality and local-processing constraints.
3. A team lead in a private company, who assigns and follows up on meeting actions.

For every interview, the team will obtain consent before recording, anonymise the respondent, store the recording outside the repository, and record the evidence ID and date in the table below. The interview guide is in [`interview-guide.md`](interview-guide.md), and the recording consent form is in [`consent-template.md`](consent-template.md).

The primary time-efficiency metric is **manual protocol preparation time**: minutes from the end of a meeting until the respondent has a usable transcript/summary with assigned actions. The interviewer will ask for the respondent's estimate for a recent comparable meeting and, where possible, measure the duration for one protocol prepared during the study. The same metric will be collected again during prototype evaluation; results will be reported as minutes per meeting, with meeting duration and language mix recorded as context.
